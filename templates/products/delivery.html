COMMON QUESTIONS

Delivery, explained.

Can't find the information you need? Contact our team.

How much does delivery cost?

Delivery costs are confirmed with you based on the arrangements available for your order and destination. Ask us for the applicable cost before finalizing your order.

How long will delivery take?

The expected delivery date or timeframe is confirmed with you during the order process. Please contact us before ordering if your delivery is time-sensitive.

Do you deliver to my location?

Contact us with your destination to confirm whether delivery can be arranged and what options are available.

Can I order several products together?

You can submit multiple products in an order request. We will confirm their availability and the applicable delivery arrangements with you.

What if a product is unavailable?

We will clarify availability with you before the order is finalized. Ask us whether an alternative or a later delivery arrangement is possible.

When is my order confirmed?

Sending an order request does not by itself confirm product availability or delivery. We will communicate the order details and applicable arrangements with you before proceeding.
